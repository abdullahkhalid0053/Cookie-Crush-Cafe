Our Bakery Story & Craft

From an obsession with precision espresso and European-butter pastry craft, Cookie Crush brings architectural warmth to every cup and crumb.

Est. 2026 • Small-Batch Roastery & Bakery

Baked with Patience, Butter & Precision

Cookie Crush was founded with a singular ambition: pairing the sleek, mood-lit energy of modern specialty coffee lounges with warm, oven-fresh cookies and celebration cakes crafted completely from scratch.

Every morning, our kitchen browns European cultured butter, chops 70% Belgian dark chocolate, and dials in single-origin Arabica shots so every visit feels intentional and memorable.
