Our Bakery Story & Craft

From a small neighborhood oven to a beloved artisan bakery and espresso bar, every Cookie Crush recipe is crafted from scratch.

Est. 2026 • Small-Batch Bakery

Baked with Patience, Butter & Passion

Cookie Crush was founded with one simple belief: the best memories happen over warm, freshly baked cookies, decadent celebration cakes, and specialty coffee brewed to perfection.

Every morning before dawn, our bakers brown European cultured butter, hand-chop Belgian dark chocolate blocks, and whip fresh vanilla bean frosting so every tray leaves our ovens golden and aromatic.
